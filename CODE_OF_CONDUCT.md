# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer at visveshkhanna@gmail.com. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions, or ban participants, who don't follow this code.
